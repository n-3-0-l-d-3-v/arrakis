# Code of Conduct

ARRAKIS is a place to learn how computers work by taking them apart. People
arrive knowing very different amounts. Everyone should be able to ask,
disagree, and be wrong in public without being made to feel small.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Explain, don't sneer.
- Criticise code, designs and measurements, not people.
- Back technical claims with something checkable: a test, a seed, a
  number, a link.
- Accept that "no" or "not now" is a legitimate answer to a proposal, and
  give reasons when you say it.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity,
  background, or experience level.
- Sexualised language or imagery, threats, or encouraging harm.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop, or deliberately
  derailing discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and Discussions,
and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer,
[@n-3-0-l-d-3-v](https://github.com/n-3-0-l-d-3-v), using the contact
details on that GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. The
maintainer may edit or remove content, and warn, temporarily block, or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, in proportion to
what happened.

This code is written for this project, in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
